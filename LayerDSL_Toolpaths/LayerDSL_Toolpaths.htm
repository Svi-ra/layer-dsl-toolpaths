Layer DSL Toolpaths

Toolpaths built from layer names such as Pocket|tool=6|depth=8.

Reading layers…
Nothing in this job carries these names. Only toolpaths on the sheet being machined are replaced; same-named toolpaths belonging to other sheets are left alone and reported. A toolpath that will not say which sheet it belongs to is never deleted.
Turn this off for unattended runs so no dialog blocks the batch.
